---
code: E0002
name: NestingTooDeep
title: Nesting deeper than the parser's limit
spec: D
---

The parser nests brackets, blocks and expressions at most 512 levels
deep. Past that limit it reports the error and skips the rest of the
nested part instead of recursing further. The limit belongs to the
compiler, not to the grammar.

## Rationale

The limit keeps the parser from running out of stack on generated or
broken input. Real code never comes close; split the expression into
`let` bindings or functions, or generate flatter code.
